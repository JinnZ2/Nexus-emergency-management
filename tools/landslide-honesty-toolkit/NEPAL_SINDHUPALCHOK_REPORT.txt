WARNINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  ! Nepal has limited systematic landslide inventory. Most events in remote areas go unreported.
  ! 2015 Gorkha earthquake triggered thousands of landslides; many were documented but coverage is uneven.
  ! SoilGrids 250m resolution is too coarse for steep Himalayan terrain. Local soil variability is extreme.
  ! Global geology maps are extremely coarse for the Himalaya. Local geological complexity (thrust faults, metamorphic zones) is not captured.
  ! Nepal has very high seismic hazard. Earthquake-triggered landslides are a major factor not fully captured in terrain-only models.
  ! UNVALIDATED MAP: Sparse inventory coverage. Many landslide-prone areas have no documented events.
  ! Himalayan terrain is extremely complex. A 30m DEM and 250m soil data miss critical local variations.
  ! Monsoon rainfall triggers are highly localized. CHIRPS 5km data cannot capture orographic precipitation at slope scale.
